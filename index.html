🐢 Amazing Turtle Facts

⏳

Ancient Survivors

Turtles have existed for over 220 million years, outlasting the dinosaurs by tens of millions of years.

🌍

World Travelers

Leatherback sea turtles migrate up to 10,000 miles round-trip between nesting and feeding grounds.

🧭

Built-in GPS

Sea turtles detect the Earth's magnetic field to navigate across entire oceans with incredible precision.

🎂

Long Lifespan

Some tortoises live over 150 years. Jonathan the tortoise from Seychelles is believed to be 190+ years old.

🌡️

Temperature = Gender

Warmer sand produces female hatchlings; cooler sand produces males. Climate change is shifting this balance.

💚

Protect Them!

6 of 7 sea turtle species are endangered or threatened. Reducing plastic and protecting beaches saves lives.
